# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Visual creators who want to reuse the transferable style of a reference image without reproducing its subject verbatim. This includes lightweight users who do not write prompts fluently and experienced creators who need to inspect evidence, edit variables, and control the final generation prompt.

## Product Purpose

Turn a reference image into a trustworthy, editable visual recipe and use it to render a new image through the Reference -> Evidence -> Render workflow. Success means users can understand what the AI observed, preserve style while changing content, and recover or iterate without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, transparent. The product should feel like a quiet professional instrument: evidence-led, editorially clear, and technically trustworthy.

## Anti-references

- A black-box image-to-prompt tool that exposes one unexplained text blob.
- A dense enterprise control panel that makes first-time creation feel administrative.
- Generic SaaS card grids, decorative neon AI effects, heavy shadows, and gratuitous glass surfaces.
- Interfaces that imply confidence, image coordinates, or provenance the model did not actually provide.

## Design Principles

1. Show evidence before asking for trust.
2. Keep generated facts, reusable style rules, and user edits visibly separate.
3. Make the default result immediately usable while preserving expert control.
4. Preserve stable context across analysis, editing, generation, history restore, and failure recovery.
5. Use restrained hierarchy and familiar product controls so the creative task remains primary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG conformance target is declared in the current product documents. Preserve semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion-safe behavior, and existing aria-live status semantics; do not encode status or confidence through color alone.
